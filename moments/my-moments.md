# My Moments

Everything you've collected or published, with your coins by vesting stage.

**Where:** Moments tab → **My Moments** (the person icon).

## Tiles

| Tile | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Promised, not graduated yet. Coins owed on Moments still collecting. |
| **Claimable now** | Vested and unclaimed. |
| **Still vesting** | Unlocks at the monthly cliffs. |
| **Claimed** | Already in your wallet. |

**Claim All** appears when anything is claimable and mints every vested tranche across all your graduated Moments in one transaction. The sheet lists each $TICKER it will claim.

## Your Moments

One row per Moment: "N editions · promised X · claimed Y · in wallet Z" (plus "creator" if it's yours), with the right side showing claimable, pending or expired. Tap a row to open the Moment.

Empty state: *"Collect a Moment and it shows up here with its editions and coins."*

## Where else Moments show up

* **Home → My Holdings → Moments**: "N editions · 1.2M TICKER" with Graduated / Expired / N% to graduation.
* **Home** hero card: the **Moments** value (held and owed coins at the pool price; pre-graduation entitlements count as zero).
* **Portfolio → My Holdings → NFTs**: your editions as "Moment · OpenSea" tiles.
* **Portfolio → Moments**: volume, fees and activity ("Collected …", "Published …", "Withdrew proceeds", "Withdrew pool fees").
* **OpenSea**: every edition, at `opensea.io/item/monad/<NFT contract>/<edition>`. The share button on a Moment page shares the collection link with the message *"\<name\> — a Moment on Monad, kept forever. Collect it on DyorHQ, The RWA HQ for social trading."*

## Editions on other wallets

Editions are ordinary ERC-721s: you can send them or sell them on OpenSea. The **coin entitlement stays with the wallet that collected**; it does not travel with the NFT. Keep that in mind before selling an edition of a Moment that's about to graduate.
