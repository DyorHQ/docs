## In-app help

**Where:** side menu → **Get Help**, or Profile → **Support**.

| Row | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact Support** | Emails team@dyorhq.fun with the subject "DyorHQ support" |
| **Report a Bug** | Emails team@dyorhq.fun with a template (what happened, what you expected, steps to reproduce); your app version, build, iOS version and device model are appended automatically |
| **X · @DyorHQ_** | Opens the X profile |
| **dyorhq.fun** | Opens the website |
| **Terms of Use** | Opens dyorhq.fun/terms |

The footer reminds you: *"Self-custodial: support can never reach your keys or funds. Never share a recovery phrase with anyone."*

{% hint style="info" %}
📸 **Screenshot here:** Get Help screen. Suggested file: `.gitbook/assets/61-get-help.png`

## What to include in a support email

* Your wallet address (never your key or phrase)
* The transaction hash from Monadscan, if there is one
* What you tapped, what you expected, what happened
* Screenshots of any error message
